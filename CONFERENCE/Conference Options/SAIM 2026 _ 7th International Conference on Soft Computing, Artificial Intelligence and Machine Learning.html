Call For Papers

7th International Conference on Soft Computing, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (SAIM 2026)

February 14 ~ 15, 2026, Chennai, India

Scope & Topics

7th International Conference on Soft Computing, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (SAIM 2026) nvites high quality research contributions from academia and industry. SAIM has established itself as a forum for presenting cutting edge advances in AI, machine learning, soft computing, and intelligent systems.

SAIM 2026 aims to bring together leading researchers to exchange ideas, discuss innovations, and explore emerging challenges shaping the future of intelligent technologies. We welcome original, unpublished work that pushes the boundaries of theory, methods, systems, and applications.

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to, the following

    SAIM 2026 invites submissions on all areas of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Soft Computing, including but not limited to:

  • Foundations of Artificial Intelligence
  • Machine Learning Theory, Optimization and Algorithms
  • Deep Learning Architectures and Training Techniques
  • Reinforcement Learning and Decision Making
  • Probabilistic Modeling and Bayesian Methods
  • Trustworthy, Explainable and Interpretable AI
  • Fairness, Accountability, Transparency and Ethics in AI
  • Causality in Machine Learning
  • Continual, Lifelong and Transfer Learning
  • Multimodal Learning and Foundation Models
  • Generative AI (Text, Image, Audio, Video)
  • Autonomous Agents and Multi Agent Systems
  • Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition
  • 3D Vision, Scene Understanding and Reconstruction
  • Vision Language Models
  • Natural Language Processing and Large Language Models
  • Information Retrieval, Search and Web Intelligence
  • Knowledge Representation, Reasoning and Knowledge Graphs
  • Conversational AI and Dialogue Systems
  • Bioinformatics, Computational Biology and Biomedical AI
  • AI for Healthcare, Precision Medicine and Medical Imaging
  • Neurosymbolic and Cognitive Computing
  • AI for Scientific Discovery, Simulation and Modeling
  • Data Mining, Big Data Analytics and Scalable ML
  • Data Visualization and Exploratory Analysis
  • Social Network Analysis and Computational Social Science
  • E-commerce, FinTech and Smart Economy Applications
  • AI for Climate Science, Sustainability and Smart Cities
  • Distributed, Parallel and High Performance AI Systems
  • Edge AI, TinyML and Embedded Intelligence
  • Neuromorphic and Morphic Computing
  • AI Optimized Hardware and Accelerators
  • Nano/Micro Systems for Intelligent Computing
  • Soft Computing Theory and Applications
  • Fuzzy Logic, Fuzzy Systems and Rough Sets
  • Evolutionary Computing, Swarm Intelligence and Optimization
  • Hybrid Intelligent Systems
  • Robotics, Autonomous Systems and Intelligent Control
  • Human Robot Interaction and Embodied AI
  • Cyber Physical Systems and Intelligent Networks
  • AI Security, Privacy and Adversarial Machine Learning
  • Robustness, Safety and Reliability of AI Systems
  • Quantum Machine Learning
  • Benchmarking, Evaluation and Reproducibility in AI
  • AI for Creativity, Art, Media and Cultural Analytics

Paper Submission

Authors are invited to submit papers through the conference Submission System by January 31, 2026 (Final Call). Submissions must be original and should not have been published previously or be under consideration for publication while being evaluated for this conference. The proceedings of the conference will be published by Computer Science Conference Proceedings in Computer Science & Information Technology (CS & IT) series (Confirmed).

Selected papers from SAIM 2026, after further revisions, will be published in the special issue of the following journals.

  • International Journal on Soft Computing, Artificial Intelligence and Applications (IJSCAI)
  • International Journal on Soft Computing (IJSC)
  • International Journal of Artificial Intelligence & Applications (IJAIA)
  • Advances in Vision Computing: An International Journal (AVC)
  • Machine Learning and Applications: An International Journal (MLAIJ)
