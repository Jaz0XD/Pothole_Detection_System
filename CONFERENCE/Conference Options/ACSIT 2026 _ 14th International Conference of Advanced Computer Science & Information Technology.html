Call For Papers

14th International Conference of Advanced Computer Science & Information Technology (ACSIT 2026)

February 14 ~ 15, 2026, Chennai, India

Scope & Topics

14th International Conference of Advanced Computer Science & Information Technology (ACSIT 2026) is a leading international forum for presenting innovative research, emerging technologies, and significant advances across the foundations and applications of computer science. The conference brings together researchers, industry professionals, and practitioners to exchange ideas, discuss current challenges, and explore future directions in the field.

ACSIT 2026 invites high quality submissions showcasing original research results, innovative projects, survey studies, and industrial experiences that contribute to the advancement of computer science and information technology. Through keynote talks, technical sessions, and collaborative discussions, the conference aims to foster knowledge sharing, strengthen academic industry connections, and promote impactful innovation.

Paper Submission

Authors are invited to submit papers through the conference Submission System by January 31, 2026(Final Call). Submissions must be original and should not have been published previously or be under consideration for publication while being evaluated for this conference. The proceedings of the conference will be published by Computer Science Conference Proceedings in Computer Science & Information Technology (CS & IT) series (Confirmed).

Selected papers from ACSIT 2026, after further revisions, will be published in the special issues of the following journals.

  • Computer Science & Engineering: An International Journal (CSEIJ)
  • International Journal of Computer Science & Information Technology (IJCSIT) - INSPEC Indexed
  • International Journal of Information Technology Convergence and Services (IJITCS)
  • International Journal of Computer Science, Engineering and Applications (IJCSEA)
  • International Journal of Advanced Information Technology (IJAIT)
  • International Journal of Information Sciences and Techniques (IJIST)
  • International Journal in Foundations of Computer Science & Technology (IJFCST)
  • International Journal on Computational Science & Applications (IJCSA)
  • International Journal on Cybernetics & Informatics (IJCI)
  • International Journal of Computer Science and Engineering Survey (IJCSES)
  • Advanced Computing: An International Journal (ACIJ)
